Is the shelf price a good deal?

Snap the price tag. StickerCheck reads the price and product, then shows what the same item costs online, store by store.

Used & refurbished

eBay, Back Market, Swappa, Mercari and other resale prices for electronics and fashion.

Price match card

No account

Your scans stay on your iPhone. No sign-in, no tracking unless you allow it.

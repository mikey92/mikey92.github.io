Offshot Support

Delete screenshots

  1. Allow Photos access. You can choose limited access.
  2. Select screenshots you want to remove.
  3. Review your selection and the deletion warning.
  4. Confirm the deletion request, then return to the screenshot list.

Recover deleted screenshots

Open Recently Deleted in Photos to check whether an item can be recovered. Items can usually be recovered for 30 days, with exceptions. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions may sync to other devices. Offshot does not provide its own undo or permanently erase items from Recently Deleted. Apple’s deletion and recovery guidance.

Purchases and ads

To restore lifetime ad removal, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the product or App Store is unavailable, close the purchase screen and try again later. You do not need to buy it again.

An ad may appear after a completed deletion. If an ad is unavailable, fails to load, or cannot connect, deletion still completes. Use the ad’s AdChoices or information button to report an ad to Google. You do not need to click the ad content. Advertising Privacy Choices appears in Settings when required by Google.

Get help

Ask a question or report a problem on our support tracker. Include the app version, iOS version, and steps that led to the issue.

The support tracker is public and is provided by GitHub, which may require its own account. Please do not include private screenshots, personal information, installation identifiers, receipts, or payment details. The app itself does not require an account.

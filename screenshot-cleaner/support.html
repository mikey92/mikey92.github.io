Screenshot Cleaner Support

How to clean up

  1. Allow Photos access. You can choose limited access.
  2. Select screenshots you want to remove.
  3. Review your selection and the deletion warning.
  4. Confirm the deletion request. Return to Screenshots when the cleanup completes.

Where deleted screenshots go

Open Photos → Recently Deleted to check whether an item can be recovered. Items can usually be recovered for 30 days, with exceptions. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions may sync to other devices. Screenshot Cleaner does not provide its own undo or permanently erase items from Recently Deleted. Apple’s deletion and recovery guidance.

Purchases and ads

Use Settings → Restore Purchases to restore a verified lifetime ad-removal purchase through the Apple Account that made it. If the product or App Store is unavailable, close the purchase screen and try again later. Do not buy again simply to restore a purchase.

Ads are offered only when available after a completed cleanup. No-fill, offline, or failed ads do not stop deletion. Use an ad’s AdChoices or information control for Google’s reporting options; you do not need to click the ad content. Advertising Privacy Choices appears in Settings when required by Google.

Get help

Ask a question or report a problem on our support tracker. Include the app version, iOS version, and steps that led to the issue.

The support tracker is public and is provided by GitHub, which may require its own account. Please do not include private screenshots, personal information, installation identifiers, receipts, or payment details. The app itself does not require an account.

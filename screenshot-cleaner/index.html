Review before deleting

  • Browse screenshots and select one or several.
  • Review before requesting a deletion through Photos.
  • Use the app without creating an account.
  • Keep screenshot images and content on your device.

Ads and one-time ad removal

The free version may show an ad after a successful deletion. A batch counts as one deletion. If an ad is unavailable, deletion still completes. Lifetime ad removal is planned at $1.99 in the U.S., paid once. The app shows the App Store price when the product is available. Restore Purchases is included.

Ads, purchases, and anonymous usage sharing use the internet. Read how data is handled.

Deleting screenshots changes your Photos library. With iCloud Photos, deletions may sync to your other devices. You can usually recover items in Photos, under Recently Deleted, for 30 days.
